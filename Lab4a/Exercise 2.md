# Low-Calorie Veggie & Turkey Sandwich (about 300–350 calories)

## Ingredients

* 2 slices whole-grain or sprouted bread (around 70–90 cal each)
* 2–3 slices lean turkey breast (about 2 oz / 60 g)
* 2–3 tomato slices
* 3–4 cucumber slices
* A handful of baby spinach or mixed greens
* 1 tsp light mayonnaise or Dijon mustard
* A squeeze of lemon juice
* Optional: pinch of black pepper or fresh herbs (basil, dill)

## Method

1. Lightly toast the bread if you like extra crunch.
2. Spread light mayo or Dijon on one slice. **Drizzle lemon juice over the greens for a fresh kick.**
3. Layer turkey, tomatoes, cucumber, and spinach.
4. Season with black pepper or herbs, then top with the second slice of bread.

This sandwich is high in protein and fiber while staying light on calories—perfect for a quick, healthy meal.
